Hi! I am Yue, a master's graduate in Computer Science from the University of Copenhagen, with a focus on Human-Computer Interaction.

My interests include Animal-Computer Interaction, Social and Collaborative Computing, and Human-AI Interaction.

I am particularly interested in how interactive systems shape people's understanding, experiences, and relationships in everyday settings, and I enjoy combining empirical studies with design and prototyping.
